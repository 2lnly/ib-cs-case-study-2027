Overview from the booklet & subject brief

Visionary Studios is a creative design company. It is evaluating generative AI to produce high-quality images for advertising campaigns, concept art and digital media, and is weighing diffusion models, GANs and hybrid models against each other.

The company assesses each model on five factors: output quality, computational efficiency, training stability, flexibility and scalability, and consistency (character consistency and style adherence).

It also has to handle three ethical and legal concerns:

  1. Dataset curation: avoid copyrighted material and respect intellectual property law.
  2. Bias and fairness: check datasets for bias that leads to exclusionary or inaccurate outputs.
  3. Transparency and AI disclosure: set clear rules for disclosing AI-generated content.

The GAN section (booklet lines 48–59) and the hybrid models section (lines 60–75) are HL only.

Glossary booklet terminology · plain-language summaries

SLHL Both levels

Diffusion model

Generates images by starting from random noise and removing it step by step with a trained denoiser.

Denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM)

The framework modern diffusion models build on. It formalises adding noise (forward process) and learning to remove it (reverse process).

Denoising

One step of predicting and removing noise from an image. Generation repeats this many times.

Noise injection

Introducing random noise: as the starting point for generation, and when corrupting images during training.

Convolutional neural network (CNN)

A neural network that uses learned filters over local image regions. It is the usual denoiser, often a U-Net.

Text-to-image generation

Creating images from a written prompt, as in DALL·E and Stable Diffusion.

Conditional image generation

Generation steered by an input such as a class label, sketch, segmentation map or text.

Unconditional image generation

Generation with no guiding input, drawing only on patterns learned from the data. Useful for exploration and synthetic datasets.

Image-to-image translation

Turning one kind of image into another, such as sketch to render or greyscale to colour.

Segmentation map

An image where every pixel is labelled with a region or class (sky, road, person). It can serve as a layout for generation.

Character consistency

Keeping a recurring character or motif looking the same across many generated images.

Embedding-based approach

Representing a character or style as a learned vector (embedding) that is reused in prompts to keep outputs consistent.

Training stability

Whether training converges reliably rather than oscillating, failing or producing repetitive outputs.

Dataset curation

Selecting, filtering and documenting training data, for example to exclude copyrighted or harmful material.

Bias mitigation

Steps to detect and reduce unfair or unrepresentative patterns in data and outputs.

HL Higher level only

Generative adversarial network (GAN)

Two networks trained against each other: a generator makes fake images and a discriminator tries to spot them.

Generator

Turns a random noise vector into a candidate image, and learns by fooling the discriminator.

Discriminator

A classifier that labels images as real (from the training set) or fake (from the generator).

Adversarial dynamic

The competition between the two networks. It drives sharp images, but if either side gets too strong, training becomes unstable.

D-dimensional noise vector

The generator's input: a list of D random numbers. Each different vector produces a different image.

Mode collapse

The generator produces only a few kinds of output instead of the full variety in the data.

Variational autoencoder (VAE)

Encodes images into a structured latent space and decodes samples from it. Flexible for exploration, but often blurrier.

Latent space

A compressed representation in which similar images sit close together, so moving through it changes the image smoothly.

Flow-based model

Learns an exact, reversible mapping between noise and images, which makes generation traceable and reproducible.

Hybrid model

Combines approaches, for example a VAE latent space with diffusion (Stable Diffusion) or a diffusion model with GAN training, to offset each one's weaknesses.
